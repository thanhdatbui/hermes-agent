# Interpreting the Watchdog Follow Chéo Report

When interpreting or explaining the Telegram report block `Follow chéo` generated by `feed_session_watchdog.py`:

## 1. Header Line Semantics
- `• Follow chéo (N lượt follow) [Module 2 (Anchor): A | Module 1 (Bù): B]`:
  - `N lượt follow`: Total count of entries in the merged `followed` lists across all machines in the cluster for that shift/row.
  - `Module 2 (Anchor)`: Count of follows performed on Anchor accounts (profile engagement & follow on video).
  - `Module 1 (Bù)`: Count of ladder follows (following from Anchor's Following list).
  - A machine may record follow events in `followed` but later fail verification or release checks.

## 2. Success vs Nhả Follow Discrepancy
- Why `(N lượt follow)` can be non-zero while `Success (0): Không có`:
  - `Success (X máy)` represents machines where follow succeeded AND was retained without trigger of release/rollback (`FOLLOW_FAILED` with `follow_failed: True`).
  - If a machine followed 1 or more accounts, but then during in-session verification (Path B / spot-check) TikTok rolled back/released the follow, that machine is categorized under `Nhả follow`, removing it from `Success`.
  - The aggregate count at the top records attempts that completed before rollback detection; `Success` records machines that ended cleanly.

## 3. Subgroups of Nhả Follow
- `Nhả liền (0 lượt - X máy)`: Follow failed/released immediately on the very first attempt (canary follow).
- `1 - 4 lượt (Y máy)`: Succeeded 1 to 4 follows before detecting a release.
- `5 - 9 lượt` / `10+ lượt`: Reached higher counts before spot-check detected a rollback.
- Every machine in `Nhả follow` enters per-nick cooldown (`follow_released_daily_cooldown`).

## 4. Lỗi Script / Xác Minh
- Failures due to UI navigation or timing (e.g., `mở tab Đã follow fail cho <anchor> sau ladder (lần 2)`).
- These are distinct from silent follow releases; they indicate failures in locating or clicking specific elements/tabs or transient timeouts.

## 5. Bỏ Qua (Safe Gating)
- **Đang dưỡng sinh**: Organic Rest policy (~33% of fleet randomly assigned to browse feed only, no follows, no uploads).
- **Chưa đủ 10 video**: Video Gate enforcement. Accounts with `< 10` posted videos are skipped to protect trust score and prevent immediate follow rollbacks.
- **Khác**: Missing account mapping, warmup phase rules, or sensitive skips.
